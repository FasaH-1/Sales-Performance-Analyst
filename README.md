# Sales Performance Analysis — Semester 1

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Semester 1 sales performance through an interactive dashboard and business performance reporting. The analysis covers revenue achievement, Closed Won performance, regional contributions, product performance, and sales pipeline gaps.

The objective is to identify performance drivers, highlight areas requiring improvement, and propose process improvements to support data-driven sales management.

**Dashboard:** [View Sales Performance Dashboard](https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/142fe59a-8997-4193-adae-69549c6787cd)

## Objectives

- Monitor sales performance against established targets.
- Evaluate revenue and Closed Won achievement across regions.
- Identify top-performing products, product categories, and sales opportunities.
- Analyze gaps between revenue performance and Closed Won results.
- Recommend workflow automation to improve data consistency and operational efficiency.

## Key Findings

### 1. Regional Performance

Kalimantan recorded the strongest revenue performance, achieving **166.92% of its target** and contributing **34.5% of total revenue**. In contrast, Jawa achieved 70.57% of its revenue target, indicating a need for closer performance monitoring.

### 2. Product Performance

Managed Network generated the highest revenue among product categories at IDR 1.18 billion, followed by Cyber Security at IDR 1.11 billion. At the individual product level, Hybrid Storage Suite contributed IDR 630 million.

### 3. Closed Won Performance

Despite revenue achievement exceeding its target, overall Closed Won achievement reached only 72.08%. This gap highlights the need to review opportunity tracking, sales-stage updates, and the consistency of pipeline reporting.

### 4. Closed Lost Analysis

Closed Lost opportunities were concentrated in Cyber Security, Collaboration Tools, and Data Analytics. These findings can help prioritize opportunity reviews and identify areas for improving sales conversion.

## Proposed Business Improvements

- **Automated Revenue Detection:** Match project IDs between prospect and revenue records to trigger Closed Won status updates automatically.
- **Contract Digitalization:** Streamline contract creation, legal review, approval, and customer delivery to reduce manual coordination.
- **Pipeline Monitoring:** Improve visibility into opportunity stages, revenue gaps, and sales performance across regions.
- **Opportunity Prioritization:** Focus on high-potential prospects to improve conversion opportunities and support revenue growth.
